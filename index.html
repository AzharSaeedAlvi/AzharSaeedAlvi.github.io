About

The short, honest version

Two years in, I've learned that most "technical" problems are really translation problems — between a client's intent and a platform's config, between a failing request and the log that explains it.

I'm a Senior Product Consultant at Cvent, working with enterprise SaaS clients on event-technology platforms (iCapture and Jifflenow). Day to day, I set up integrations between iCapture and clients' CRMs using pre-built templates, troubleshoot configuration issues, and diagnose failures with Postman and .HAR log analysis. I started as an Associate here and have since moved up to Senior.

Left to my own devices, I design and ship internal AI agents and browser automations that the team now uses every day. I define the requirements and product decisions, direct AI to generate the code, then test and assemble it into something that actually works. That's the lane I'm growing into — Solutions / Implementation Engineering.
